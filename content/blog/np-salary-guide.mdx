## The national picture

The Bureau of Labor Statistics puts the median annual wage for nurse practitioners at $129,210 (BLS OEWS, May 2024). Just as important as the level is the trajectory: BLS Employment Projections show nurse practitioner employment growing about 40% from 2024 to 2034 — among the fastest of any occupation the bureau tracks. Sustained demand is the quiet force behind NP salary growth, because employers competing for a scarce clinician pool compete on compensation first.

A median is a starting point, not a quote. Actual offers depend on where you practice, what setting you work in, and how the role is structured.

## The five factors that move NP pay

**1. State and cost of living.** State-level averages differ substantially, and high-paying states are often high-cost states. A smaller number in a low-cost state can out-earn a bigger number in a coastal metro once housing is priced in. Our [salary guide](/salary-guide) computes live averages for every state from current postings.

**2. Practice setting.** Inpatient, emergency, and acute-care roles typically price above outpatient primary care, reflecting acuity, shift patterns, and call. Shift differentials, weekend premiums, and on-call stipends stack on top of base salary in hospital settings.

**3. Specialty and certification.** Certified registered nurse anesthetists sit at the top of the APRN pay scale — postings typically run $180K–$250K+ — while high-demand prescriber specialties also command premiums. See our comparison of the [highest-paying NP specialties](/blog/highest-paying-np-specialties).

**4. Employment structure.** A full-time W-2 role in the typical $110K–$170K band bundles health insurance, 401(k) match, CME allowance, malpractice coverage, and PTO. Independent-contractor (1099) roles pay $60–$150+ per hour but shift taxes, malpractice, and benefits onto you — the headline rate is not take-home. Our [1099 vs W-2 breakdown](/blog/np-1099-vs-w2) walks through the math.

**5. Practice authority.** In the 27 states + DC that grant full practice authority (AANP State Practice Environment, 2025), NPs can practice and prescribe independently. FPA widens your options — independent practice, telehealth panels, practice ownership — and options are negotiating leverage.

## How to use salary data in a negotiation

Anchor on evidence, not instinct. Bring the BLS median for your state, the live posting averages for your setting, and two or three comparable open roles. Then negotiate the whole package: base, productivity or quality bonuses, CME budget, licensure and certification reimbursement, PTO, and call expectations. A $5K base difference can be dwarfed by a weaker retirement match or an extra weekend rotation.

If you're comparing a W-2 offer against contract or per-diem rates, net out the benefits the contractor self-funds before concluding the hourly rate "pays more."

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average nurse practitioner salary?

The U.S. median annual wage for nurse practitioners is $129,210 (BLS OEWS, Nurse Practitioners 29-1171, May 2024). Typical full-time postings on this board fall in a $110K–$170K band, with new-grad roles commonly starting at $95K–$120K.

### Which nurse practitioners get paid the most?

Certified registered nurse anesthetists (CRNAs) carry the highest APRN pay bands, typically $180K–$250K+. Among NP roles, inpatient, emergency, and acute-care settings generally price above outpatient primary care.

### Is NP a growing career?

Yes. BLS Employment Projections show nurse practitioner employment growing about 40% from 2024 to 2034, among the fastest-growing occupations tracked by the bureau.

### Do nurse practitioners earn more in full practice authority states?

Full practice authority doesn't guarantee a higher base salary, but the 27 states + DC with FPA (AANP, 2025) give NPs more earning paths — independent practice, telehealth panels, and practice ownership — which strengthens negotiating position.

### How accurate are salary ranges in job postings?

Treat posted ranges as an opening position. Cross-check them against the live state averages in our salary guide, which are computed from all current postings with disclosed pay, and against the BLS median for your state.
