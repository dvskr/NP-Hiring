## When is National Nurse Practitioner Week 2026?

National Nurse Practitioner Week 2026 is November 8 to 14, 2026. AANP states the dates on its official site: "During National Nurse Practitioner Week, November 8-14, 2026, we celebrate these health care professionals," in its [NP Week press materials](https://npweek.aanp.org/press-releases-and-letters), and its [local activities page](https://npweek.aanp.org/ideas-for-local-activities) gives the same week. November 8, 2026 is a Sunday, so the week runs Sunday to Saturday.

One fixed event is already announced. AANP's [NP Week home page](https://npweek.aanp.org/) invites NPs and NP students to gather, in white coats and with signs, outside the Today Show on Tuesday, November 10.

AANP's NP Week pages, opened on October 6, 2026, name a week rather than a single "National Nurse Practitioner Day," and the pages we opened show no 2026 theme. Treat the week itself as the observance.

## What is National Nurse Practitioner Week, and who leads it?

National Nurse Practitioner Week is an annual week in November that recognizes nurse practitioners, and AANP leads it through a dedicated site at [npweek.aanp.org](https://npweek.aanp.org/). A nurse practitioner (NP) is one of four advanced practice registered nurse (APRN) roles: a registered nurse with graduate preparation who assesses, diagnoses and treats patients within a population focus such as family or psychiatric mental health. Our explainer on [what an APRN is](/blog/what-is-an-aprn) covers the four APRN roles.

AANP's 2026 materials frame the week around the profession's history: "Sixty years after the first NP program launched in 1965, the profession has grown to include more than 461,000 licensed NPs nationwide" ([AANP NP Week press materials](https://npweek.aanp.org/press-releases-and-letters)).

The official site is a toolkit more than an event listing. According to [AANP](https://npweek.aanp.org/), it offers a sample news release, talking points, an NP fact sheet, community activity ideas, a media interview guide, posters, and guidelines for writing a letter to the editor. AANP's [activities page](https://npweek.aanp.org/ideas-for-local-activities) asks participants to use the hashtags #NPWeek and #NPsLead, to share stories at aanp.org/shareyourstory, and to ask local or state officials for proclamations using AANP's template. Signed proclamations appear on AANP's [NP Week proclamation map](https://npweek.aanp.org/proclamation).

Three notes keep these numbers honest. The $132,300 figure is a median from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) survey for May 2025, not an average, and pay varies widely by state, setting and specialty; the [NP salary guide](/salary-guide) breaks it down. The 41% figure comes from the BLS Employment Projections release of August 27, 2026 ([USDL-26-1422](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecopro.nr0.htm)), which says NPs are "projected to experience the fastest employment growth of all detailed occupations (+41.0 percent)." It is a national projection, not a forecast for any one market; our [NP job outlook](/blog/nurse-practitioner-job-outlook) explains how to read it.

Full practice authority is one of the state practice environments AANP tracks; our [full practice authority guide](/resources/fpa-guide) explains what it covers. Many full practice states still require transition-to-practice hours first, so check the state rules on our [scope of practice reference](/scope-of-practice) before describing any one state.

## How can employers recognize NPs during NP Week?

Employers recognize NPs best by giving them something that changes their working year, not just their week. A meal or a gift is a fine gesture, but the ideas below address the parts of the job NPs live with all year: time, development, workload and being heard.

1. **Protect CME time and fund it.** Confirm each NP's continuing education allowance and protected days for the coming year, and say so publicly during the week. An allowance without time to use it is half a benefit.
2. **Recognize NPs by name, in public.** Name each NP and what they built this year in an all staff message, a patient newsletter or a social post using #NPWeek. Ask permission first.
3. **Hold a career development conversation.** Schedule a short one-to-one with each NP about the next two years: a new clinic line, precepting, a leadership role, a second certification. Write down what you agree to.
4. **Run a listening session on workload and documentation.** Ask what slows the day: template length, inbox volume, patients per day, rooming support, after-hours charting.
5. **Fix one pain point and announce it.** Pick one issue from the listening session that you can change within 30 days, such as a template, a staffing gap or a scheduling rule, and commit to it during NP Week.
6. **Pay the fees that keep NPs practicing.** Where your budget allows, cover license renewals, certification fees, DEA registration or professional dues, and state the policy in writing.
7. **Back the profession publicly.** Ask your local official for an NP Week proclamation using [AANP's template](https://npweek.aanp.org/proclamation), or invite local media to meet your NPs.

## What are good NP Week ideas for a small practice?

A small practice can do NP Week well with almost no budget by choosing two or three items and doing them properly. Where the owner or manager sets schedules and templates directly, a promised change can happen within weeks.

- **Block one protected afternoon** for each NP during the week or the month after, for CME, charting catch-up or a project of their choice.
- **Put the NP's name and credentials on your website and in your patient portal** if they are not already there, so patients know who is treating them.
- **Ask your NPs which single task they would remove** from their week, then remove it or delegate it.
- **Write a short note** to each NP describing a specific contribution.
- **Use AANP's free materials:** put up an NP Week poster from the [NP Week toolkit](https://npweek.aanp.org/), or post about your own team using #NPWeek.

If you employ certified registered nurse anesthetists (CRNAs), certified nurse-midwives (CNMs) or clinical nurse specialists (CNSs), their roles have their own recognition weeks, listed below. Do not fold them into NP Week.

## How can employers use NP Week in recruiting?

NP Week is a good moment to recruit because NPs are paying attention to the profession, but the message has to be concrete. A thank-you post next to a vague job ad undercuts itself.

- **Post open roles before the week starts.** [Post a nurse practitioner job](/post-job) by early November so it is live while NPs are reading NP Week content. Current posting terms are on the [pricing page](/pricing).
- **State the pay range.** A posted range lets NPs judge the role before they apply. NP Hiring's [pay transparency report](/reports/pay-transparency) tracks how often postings on the board state pay; see the live report for the current share.
- **Name the recognition you offer all year.** If you fund CME, protect administrative time or pay license fees, put it in the posting, not just in the NP Week post.
- **Describe the schedule honestly.** State patients per day, call, weekend coverage and documentation support plainly.
- **Use market context.** The [State of NP Hiring 2026 report](/reports/state-of-np-hiring-2026) summarizes specialty demand, geography, work mode and advertised pay across postings on NP Hiring, which helps you benchmark a role before it goes live.

For a hiring guide and job description templates, see [employer resources](/for-employers/resources) or the [employer overview](/for-employers).

## What employers should do now

As of mid October 2026, employers have about four weeks before November 8, which is enough for recognition that is more than a lunch. A short checklist:

1. **By October 20:** confirm next year's CME allowance and protected days for each NP, and decide on one fee or dues policy you can announce.
2. **By late October:** schedule a listening session and the one-to-one career conversations, and request a local proclamation if you plan to.
3. **By November 1:** publish open NP roles with a pay range and a plain description of schedule and support.
4. **During November 8 to 14:** recognize NPs by name, announce the pain point you will fix, and share AANP's verified facts with their sources.
5. **Within 30 days after:** deliver the fix and tell the team it is done.

NPs reading this from the other side of the desk can use the week too: it is a natural moment to ask your manager about CME support or a career path. If the answer is not what you hoped, [browse current NP jobs](/jobs) or [set up a job alert](/job-alerts) for roles that state their pay.
