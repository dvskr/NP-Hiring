**Quick answer:** Remote and telehealth NP jobs require an active APRN license in each state where your patients are located, not where you sit. As of October 2026, Medicare telehealth flexibilities run through December 31, 2027 and DEA telemedicine prescribing flexibilities through December 31, 2026. Pay follows state, setting, specialty, and employment structure, and every added state license widens the panel you can cover.

## Remote vs telehealth: the distinction that matters

A **telehealth** role is patient-facing care by video and phone: scheduled visits, asynchronous messaging, e-prescribing, and documentation between sessions. **Remote** is the broader umbrella. It includes telehealth but also utilization review, care management, and other non-visit clinical work done from home. When you filter [remote NP jobs](/jobs/remote) or [telehealth NP jobs](/jobs/telehealth), read the day-to-day description, not just the tag. Roles away from direct care are covered in our guide to [non-clinical NP jobs](/blog/non-clinical-nurse-practitioner-jobs).

## Telehealth rules as of October 2026

Three sets of rules shape virtual NP work, each checked at its primary source on October 6, 2026.

**Medicare coverage.** [Section 6209 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ75/html/PLAW-119publ75.htm) (Public Law 119-75, signed February 3, 2026) extends the Medicare telehealth flexibilities through December 31, 2027. Until then, Medicare patients can be seen at home without the old geographic limits, audio-only visits remain available, and the in-person visit requirement for telemental health is delayed to January 1, 2028. Medicaid and commercial plans set their own telehealth rules.

**Prescribing controlled substances.** The [fourth temporary extension](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-12-31/html/2025-24123.htm) from the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) and HHS, published December 31, 2025, lets a DEA-registered practitioner prescribe Schedule II to V controlled substances by telemedicine without a prior in-person evaluation through December 31, 2026, if the other legal requirements are met. DEA sent a final special registration rule for White House review on August 25, 2026 ([McDermott Will & Schulte, September 18, 2026](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/dea-appears-close-to-finalizing-telemedicine-special-registration/)); its text is not public, so the rule after 2026 is still pending. State prescribing law also applies. Our guide to [whether NPs can prescribe controlled substances](/blog/can-nurse-practitioners-prescribe-controlled-substances) covers the details.

**Licensure across state lines.** Nurses "are required to be licensed in the state where the patient is located at the time service is provided" ([Nurse Licensure Compact FAQ](https://www.nursecompact.com/FAQs.page)). The Nurse Licensure Compact (NLC) "pertains to RN and LPN/VN licenses only," and an APRN must hold an individual APRN license in each state of APRN practice. The separate APRN Compact is not active: it takes effect once seven states enact it, and South Dakota became the fourth in February 2024 ([NCSBN](https://www.ncsbn.org/news/south-dakota-enacts-aprn-compact)). Our [APRN Compact states guide](/blog/aprn-compact-states) tracks its status.

In practice, a telehealth panel covering patients in four states means four APRN licenses. Budget real lead time for endorsement applications before your start date, and use the [licensure checker](/tools/licensure-checker) or our [state license guides](/resources) for each state's path.

## What the home setup requires

Employers standardize on a HIPAA-compliant workspace: a private room where screens and conversations cannot be observed, a reliable high-speed connection, and employer-approved software for the EHR and video platform. Larger platforms often ship hardware or pay an equipment stipend; smaller ones may expect your own. Ask, because it changes your effective compensation.

## What remote and telehealth NP roles pay

Remote and telehealth roles are priced by the same factors as on-site roles: the state your patients are in, the setting, the specialty, and whether the role is W-2 or 1099. The U.S. median for nurse practitioners is $132,300 (BLS OEWS, May 2025). Telehealth platforms often pay per visit or hourly, and independent-contractor (1099) arrangements are common, with the usual caveats on taxes, malpractice, and benefits (see our [1099 vs W-2 comparison](/resources/1099-vs-w2)). For state figures, use the [salary guide](/salary-guide).

Demand is durable: BLS projects about 41% employment growth for nurse practitioners from 2025 to 2035, and virtual care lets employers hire wherever the licenses are.

## How to position yourself

1. **Anchor on your home-state license first.** If you live in a compact state, secure the multistate RN license; then build APRN licenses in your target coverage states.
2. **Document your telehealth competencies.** Video-visit experience, asynchronous triage, e-prescribing workflows, and EHR fluency belong on the resume explicitly.
3. **Mind practice authority.** [Full-practice-authority states](https://www.aanp.org/advocacy/state/state-practice-environment) (27 states + DC per AANP, 2026) simplify a telehealth employer's supervision logistics, although several require transition-to-practice hours before an NP practices independently. Check each state in our [scope of practice reference](/scope-of-practice).

## Questions to ask a remote employer before accepting

1. **Licensure support:** which states will you cover, and does the employer pay for and process added APRN licenses and DEA registrations?
2. **Malpractice coverage:** does the policy cover every state on your panel, and who pays for tail coverage if you leave?
3. **Prescribing policy:** does the platform prescribe controlled substances by telemedicine, and what is its plan for January 1, 2027?
4. **Patient volume:** expected visits per hour, documentation time, and after-hours messaging duties.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can nurse practitioners work remotely?

Yes. Telehealth visits, utilization review, and care management are all done from home. Patient-facing telehealth requires an APRN license in each state where your patients are located, plus a private, HIPAA-compliant workspace. Browse current [remote NP jobs](/jobs/remote) and [telehealth NP jobs](/jobs/telehealth) to see how employers describe the work.

### Do telehealth NPs need a license in every state?

They need one in every state where their patients are located at the time of the visit, not only where the NP lives. The Nurse Licensure Compact covers RN and LPN/VN licenses only, and the APRN Compact is not active, so each APRN license is still issued state by state.

### Can telehealth NPs prescribe controlled substances?

Through December 31, 2026, a DEA-registered practitioner may prescribe Schedule II to V controlled substances by telemedicine without a prior in-person evaluation, if other federal and state requirements are met (DEA and HHS fourth temporary extension). As of October 2026, the final special registration rule that would apply afterward is pending and not public.

### Will Medicare keep covering telehealth visits?

Yes, through December 31, 2027. The Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026 (Public Law 119-75) extended the Medicare telehealth flexibilities, including home visits and audio-only care, to that date and delayed the in-person requirement for telemental health to January 1, 2028.

### How much do telehealth NPs make?

Telehealth pay is set by state, setting, specialty, and employment structure, not by the remote label. The national NP median is $132,300 (BLS OEWS, May 2025). 1099 telehealth work is quoted hourly before self-funded taxes, malpractice, and benefits, so net those out before comparing it to a W-2 salary.

### What is the outlook for telehealth NP work?

Nurse practitioner employment overall is projected to grow about 41% from 2025 to 2035 (BLS Employment Projections), and telehealth lets employers recruit across state lines, so multi-state-licensed NPs are in demand.
